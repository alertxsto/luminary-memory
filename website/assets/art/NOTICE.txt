LUMINARY MEMORY — LUNAR GARDEN ARTWORK

The lunar-*.png images are photographic/engraved artwork, composited, cropped,
recolored blue and reduced to three tones with ordered dithering for this site.
They are NOT rendered from procedural ASCII icons.

Moon photograph: Gregory H. Revera, FullMoon2010.
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FullMoon2010.jpg
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
Changes: grayscale crop, resizing, compositing with botanical engraving,
three-tone blue palette, ordered dithering and theme-specific color mapping.

Botanical engraving: Ernst Haeckel, Filicinae, plate 92,
Kunstformen der Natur (1904). Public-domain original work.
Reproduction source:
https://collectionofbeauty.com/artwork/kunstformen-images-haeckel-filicinae-92
https://assets.collectionofbeauty.com/kunstformen-images/Haeckel_Filicinae_92/1280.webp
Changes: cropped, resized, grayscale-adjusted, composited and dithered.

The resulting lunar-*.png derivative artwork is distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

Keep the source attribution, license link and change notice when redistributing.

Rebuild with Pillow and NumPy in the project environment:
python scripts/build-website-art.py --fern-source path/to/Haeckel_Filicinae_92.webp
The moon source is already tracked at website/assets/full-moon.jpg.
GutsAI artwork was studied as a visual reference; none of its image assets is
redistributed by this website.
